How it works

Three calm steps.

No tee to plant and no math in your head. Walk the putt, look at the read, then trust your hands.

01

Scan the green

Frame the putt and move with it. Pro iPhones use LiDAR and ARKit to rebuild the surface. Every other iPhone still gets a camera read.

02

See the break

A line runs from the ball to the hole, with an aim point beside it and contour bands over the slope. The tilt stops hiding.

03

Roll it in

Pace comes from that slope and the green’s stimp. Faster green, softer hands. If a figure is a model and not a measurement, the app says so.

Questions

Before you ask the group chat.

Which iPhones does it support?

Puttriot is an iPhone app. LiDAR models — iPhone Pro and Pro Max, from iPhone 12 Pro on — use ARKit scene reconstruction. Other iPhones use the camera. Same job, different sensor, and the app says which one did the work.

Does it work on a real green?

Yes. That’s the practice. Daylight and a clear look at the putt help. A living-room rug is not the point.

Can I use it in a tournament?

It’s a practice tool. Rules about measuring devices during a competition are yours to know. On the practice green, stay as long as you like.

Where does the speed suggestion come from?

The length of the putt, the slope from the scan, and how fast the green is — its stimp. A 10 is not a 13. When a figure comes from the model rather than a measurement, it’s marked that way.

Do I need an account, a clip, or a special ball?

Your iPhone. That’s the kit.
