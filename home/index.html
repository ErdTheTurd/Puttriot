Read the slope.
Draw the putt.

Puttriot uses LiDAR and ARKit to map a putting green, then draws the putt line and the aim point on the grass in front of you.

Live reads need a LiDAR iPhone Pro, from the 12 Pro on, or a LiDAR iPad Pro. Other iPhones and iPads get the demo green and the speed tools. They do not scan a real green.

Built in El Paso by Edward Dunn, a junior who plays the U.S. Kids Golf El Paso Local Tour with his brother.

Plate 02 · Devices

LiDAR, or it does not read the green.

A live read is the walk on real grass. It needs the scanner. The demo green and the speed tools are the rest of the app, and they do not pretend to be a scan.

Device What you get
iPhone Pro with LiDAR, from the 12 Pro on, including Pro Max Live read, demo green, and speed tools.
iPad Pro with LiDAR (2020 models and later) Live read, demo green, and speed tools. A 2018 iPad Pro has no LiDAR, so it does not get the live read.
Other iPhones and iPads Demo green and speed tools only. No live read. No camera scan of a real green.
Android Not supported.

A 12 Pro is enough. It does not have to be this year’s phone. The long version, including what to do when a scan looks wrong, is on Support.

Plate 03 · The walk

Set the ball. Walk to the hole.

  1. 01

    Set the ball

    The read starts there. A real ball, or a coin if you are only practicing the look.

  2. 02

    Walk slowly toward the hole

    Point the phone at the ground, not at the flag. Cover the ground the ball will cross, and a little to either side.

  3. 03

    Tap Find hole

    When you reach the cup, mark it. The line runs from the ball to that point.

  4. 04

    Take the line

    Puttriot draws the putt and the aim point on the green. Look at the putt yourself before you hit it.

That sequence is the live read. It needs LiDAR. Indoors, the demo green is the way to learn the screen. How a walk goes wrong.

Plate 04 · Speed

A preset, then the weather.

Stimp is the ramp test: how far a ball rolls on a flat green. Puttriot does not run that test. A scan of the slope is not a reading of the grass speed.

You pick a green-speed preset, or you type a number you trust. If the shop posts a stimp, use that. A fast number you typed because it sounded serious is how putts come up short.

Apple WeatherKit can nudge the speed for the weather. The app rounds your location to about 1 kilometer and asks Apple for the forecast. That is the only location use. It is not a map of your rounds.

You can ignore the nudge. If you turn location off, the preset you picked stays put.

Weather data provided by Apple Weather.

Demo green

It works indoors, and on iPhones and iPads without LiDAR. A place to learn the screen. Not a scan of the room you are standing in.
