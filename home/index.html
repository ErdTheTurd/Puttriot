In App Review · El Paso

Read this green.

Puttriot maps the surface with LiDAR while you walk slowly toward the hole, phone pointed at the ground.

It measures the slope. Break comes from that slope plus green speed: a stimp you pick, or one adjusted for today’s weather. Then it draws the line and the aim point on the grass.

Coming soon to the App Store

A live read needs a LiDAR iPhone Pro, 12 Pro or later, or an iPad Pro. Other iPhones and iPads get the demo green and the speed tools.

Built in El Paso by Edward Dunn, a junior who plays the U.S. Kids Golf El Paso Local Tour with his brother.

The walk

The phone stays on the ground.

Set the ball. Walk slowly toward the hole. Point the phone at the grass, not at the flag. LiDAR and ARKit build the surface along that walk.

The slope is the measurement. Green speed is separate. It is stimp, the distance a ball rolls on a flat green. You choose a preset, or Apple WeatherKit adjusts it for the weather where you are. That forecast uses an approximate location, rounded to about 1 kilometer.

From the slope and that speed, Puttriot draws the line and the aim point on the green in front of you.

Weather data provided by Apple Weather.

The longer version of the walk is on Support.

Green speed

Stimp is not the slope.

The screen in this picture is set to 10.0, Medium. Presets are Slow, Medium, Fast, and Tour. A slider runs from Shaggy to Augusta when you want a finer number.

“Check today’s weather” asks Apple WeatherKit to adjust that speed. It does not roll a stimpmeter. If the shop posts a number, use that. You can ignore the weather adjustment and keep the preset.

Weather data provided by Apple Weather.

Devices

LiDAR, or no live read.

iPhone Pro with LiDAR
12 Pro and later, including Pro Max. Live read, demo green, and speed tools.
iPad Pro with LiDAR
2020 models and later. Same live read. A 2018 iPad Pro has no scanner, so it does not get one.
Other iPhones and iPads
Demo green and speed tools. No live read, and no camera scan of a real green.
Android
Not supported.

Tour is optional: monthly, yearly, or lifetime, sold by Apple. There is no Puttriot account. Details of a bad read are on Support.
