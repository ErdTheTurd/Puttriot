How it works

Walk the putt. Get the read.

No clip-on sensor. No special ball. Frame the green, move with it, then put the phone down and hit the putt.

Scan the green

Pro iPhones rebuild the surface with LiDAR and ARKit. Other iPhones use the camera. The app says which sensor did the work.

See the break

A line from ball to hole, an aim point beside it, and contour bands over the slope so the tilt has a shape.

Pick a pace

Length, slope, and the stimp you enter feed a suggested speed. Modeled numbers are marked as models.

A sample read

Something you can stand over.

An illustration, so you can see the shape of an answer. Your green writes its own. In the app, modeled figures are labeled that way.

Putt + patriot.

Puttriot is putt and patriot. Designed and built in the United States for juniors and everyday golfers who still miss that four-footer the same way twice. The icon is a flag in the hole and the contour lines of a green. Navy, red, and white show up in the mark and the type, not as flag wallpaper.
